**Networking K1: Pin and Unpin on memory objects, and a `contiguous` flag on Allocate.**

The design is `SO:virtio-net` revision 4, §3.4, with the owner's rulings of Nov 25 (Q15 is "no"). This unit is its K1 row:
- **The ops.** `MemoryOp.Pin` and `Unpin` on `MemoryRight.Physical`. Pin is the only way to learn an object's physical base and length.
- **The rights.** `PoolRight.Physical` carries the right for what a pool allocates. The three default rights sets change so that `physical` is off unless asked for.
- **Contiguous allocations.** `AllocateFlag.Contiguous`; Pin answers only for a contiguous object.
- **The quarantine.** A pinned object never returns to its pool.
- **Refusals.** `Extend` and `SlabDonate` refuse a pinned object.
- **The builder.** Its minimum for "off by default".
- **The rest.** The consumer sweep, and §3.4's cases.
