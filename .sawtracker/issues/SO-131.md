## Description

**Unit U1 of the GPIO/PWM drivers design (SO-77): groundwork.** The design (`SO:gpio-pwm-drivers` revision 3, all review threads resolved) is the owner's as of Nov 16: every recommendation of its §11 is accepted (servo pins 41 and 42; no ramp; no per-client pin authority; board-held pins as a board input of the `esp32s3-gpio` type, added with SO-102's `-r8` board; the servo pins stated four times in the box's image, accepted). This unit lays what the GPIO driver (U2) and the PWM driver (U3) stand on. No board is needed.

**Build what §12's U1 lists:** the S3 board file's four devices (§3.1); `sosimage devices BOARD` and `sosimage declared PROCESS_TOML` (with `--saw`); the runner's case keys (`devices`, `inputs`, `declared_quota` with `quota_override`) and `blade test` in `drivers/esp32s3io/` in a full run; `drivers/esp32s3io/` with its hosted tests (§8.2); `ABI.md` §9's facts of §2 and §15c's cells; spec §12's stitcher sentence; CLAUDE.md's repo map.

**Since the design was written, SO-95's U6 has merged:** the stock root launches an image built from its description, sets each child's declared quota rows with `QuotaSet` before it gives anything, binds a process's `[build] inputs` as modules, gives device rows, carves parts and makes `[[setup]]` writes (`set`/`clear` read-modify-write, held against `0xf9c1e06f`), and `check-tree` holds every in-tree declaration to every board. Plan first: where a U1 runner key only re-does what a described image and the stock root now do for a driver case, say so and propose dropping or narrowing it, in a `CLARIFICATION REQUEST:` before building, since it changes the cases U2 and U3 are written against. Keep what a case needs that a described image cannot state (for one, a bad configuration's quota override).

**The reviewer's three notes:**
1. Footnote (b): `gpio_ll_output_disable` writes 0x100 at ESP-IDF v5.1 only; at v5.4.1 it is `gpio_ll_matrix_out_default`. Cite the version you state.
2. §4.4's plain output puts the GPIO matrix select before the IO MUX word (the order that cannot glitch the pad), wherever this unit states or tests that order.
3. The driver cases must not use `BOARD_LIMITS`, or "root sets the declared rows" loses its red case. Hold that in whatever runner support this unit adds.

**Review standard (the owner's ruling, Nov 15):** every check, refusal and stop has a case or builder or hosted test that goes red when it is removed or inverted; list the mutants. No existing transcript or image moves.

**Not in this unit:** the drivers and their protocols (U2, U3), the board stages (U4), the `gpioheld` board input (with SO-102 C), the box (SO-78).

Requested changes on patch SO-131.p1 (revision 1)

**Changes requested. The numbers are right, nothing that exists moved, and the builder's and runner's changes are held. What is short: two pairs of pads the library takes as plain GPIOs and ESP-IDF does not, three statements about the hardware that are not what their source says, and nine checks of the library that can be changed with its hosted tests green.**

**What holds.**

- **Nothing that exists moved** (f202 `manifest.py`; the whole gate and both smokes here: 1,086, 7, and 75 with 1 board-only not run): all 1,271 images of the base before SO-129.p2 and the ten files of that patch's two cases are the same bytes; the only new files are `stock_plan_rows`' (riscv32 and the S3) and `stock_plan_one_row`'s.
- **The builder and the runner, 24 mutants of mine, all red** (f216): thirteen of `device_lines`, `input_lines`, the description's `inputs` and `write_input` in the builder's tests; the input file not copied at `stock_plan_rows`' build, in a tree that had never built it (the builder's tests pass it, and a `lib.saw` an earlier build left would too); and ten of `_resolve_case`, the device rows, the child's role and the description's inputs at the new cases or at a build that stops the run.
- **The three notes on the design.**
  - Both functions are at their tags and do what §9d says: v5.1's `gpio_ll_output_disable` clears the enable and then writes `SIG_GPIO_OUT_IDX`, and v5.4.1's `gpio_ll_matrix_out_default` is that one write.
  - The select before the IO MUX word: with the two writes swapped, in the output's start, the input's and the servo's, `starts` is red each time (f215).
  - The declared rows on the S3: with root's quota step removed, the S3's `stock_root` is red at "SOS server: waiters limit=1" (f217).
- **The numbers, against ESP-IDF v5.4.1 and v5.1** (an Opus agent's table, f212; the rows named below I read in ESP-IDF myself): the five bases, every register offset and both 54-entry arrays, every field and word of `PINn`, the output select and the IO MUX, signals 73 to 80 and 256, the four interrupt sources, the LEDC layout with its 10.8 divider and its range, the two SYSTEM registers and their reset words, and the arithmetic worked by hand (50 Hz at 14 bits on the 40 MHz crystal is 12,500; 500 to 2,500 us are 410, 819, 1,229, 1,638 and 2,048 counts) all agree, as does every word the hosted tests assert.
- **The library, 234 mutants** (f213, the edits f214): 211 red in the hosted tests, 4 that do not compile, 5 that change nothing, and the 14 of point 4.

**1. Pads 19 and 20 are the USB-Serial-JTAG's, and pads 17 and 18 do not drive as the word says.** `check_pin` takes all four ("Every GPIO of the part can drive"), and §9c says GPIO is function 1 on every pad.

- **19 and 20:** ESP-IDF's `gpio_ll_func_sel` clears `USB_SERIAL_JTAG_USB_PAD_ENABLE` for these two before it selects a function (v5.4.1 `hal/esp32s3/include/hal/gpio_ll.h:339`, `USB_INT_PHY0_DM_GPIO_NUM` 19 and `DP` 20 in `io_mux_reg.h:164`; v5.1 has it too), and `gpio_ll_pullup_dis` overrides the D+ pull-up for pad 20. The library writes the IO MUX word alone. Either they are held by the chip as 43 and 44 are, or a start writes what ESP-IDF writes: yours and the owner's to choose, and §9 to say. I have not put it on a board.
- **17 and 18:** v5.4.1's `gpio_ll_set_drive_capability` swaps the two drive bits for these pads ("DRV = 1 and DRV = 2 register bits are flipped for IO17, IO18 on the target", `gpio_ll.h:424`; not in v5.1). `FUN_DRV_20MA` is 2 on every pad, so on these two it is the 10 mA setting. §9c's drive row and §9f's "The SVD alone: the drive strengths" do not have it.

**2. `word` is not "from `OUT` to `PCPU_INT`"** (`board.toml:76`, `s3_devices.saw:59`). Sixteen words from 0 are `BT_SELECT` (0x00) to `IN` (0x3C); `PCPU_INT` is at 0x5C. The part does hold both banks' levels and enables (`OUT` 0x04, `OUT1` 0x10, `ENABLE` 0x20, `ENABLE1` 0x2C), which is what it is for.

**3. Two sourcing sentences in §9.**

- §9d calls "its select to `0x100`, its IO MUX word, then its select to the signal" ESP-IDF's order. ESP-IDF writes the IO MUX function first: v5.1's `ledc_set_pin` is `gpio_hal_iomux_func_sel`, `gpio_set_direction` (whose `gpio_output_enable` connects `SIG_GPIO_OUT_IDX`), then the signal (`driver/ledc/ledc.c:565`, `driver/gpio/gpio.c:216`). The select first is this design's, for the reason the paragraph gives; the signal last is ESP-IDF's. And the direction step is v5.1's alone: v5.4.1's `_ledc_set_pin` is `gpio_func_sel`, `esp_gpio_reserve`, then the signal (`esp_driver_ledc/src/ledc.c:804`).
- §9d and §9f give the SVD alone for which `INT_ENA` bit is the NMI. Both tags have `#define GPIO_LL_NMI_INTR_ENA (BIT(1))` (`gpio_ll.h:35`).

**4. Fourteen mutants of the library pass its hosted tests** (the agent's; each applied again by me in my own tree, f215, beside a control that is red). Two are constants nothing reads, `OUT_SEL_PINS` and `PADS`. The other twelve, by check:

| the check | the edit | what it then takes or loses |
|---|---|---|
| the servos' pins are consecutive | `(servos[i].pin - lowest) as Int >= N` to `> N` | a pin exactly N above the lowest |
| a set of pins is empty | `self.low == 0 && self.high == 0` to `\|\|`, and to `self.low == 0` | a set with pins in one bank alone reads as empty |
| which bank a pin is in | `pin < 32` to `pin < 31` in `PinSet.level` | pin 31 is read from the high bank |
| every pin of a GPIO configuration is checked | `i = i + 1` to `i + 2` | every other pin |
| a servo's least pulse is not above its greatest | `min_us > max_us` to `>=` | equal ones are refused |
| the frame is a multiple of 8 | `% 8` to `% 16` | 20,008 us is refused |
| a pulse's counts round to nearest | `+ frame_us / 2` to `+ frame_us / 2 - 1` | an exact half rounds down |
| an output's level is 0 or 1 | `is_output(p.mode) &&` removed | `GpioPin.level` says an input's is 0; nothing refuses or tests another |
| the refusals' words | three of `describe()`'s lines changed (`NotAGpio`, `ConsolePin`, `PinsNotAligned`) | each still passes |

**Smaller.** `config.saw` asserts pin 33 is accepted, and on a part with octal flash ESP-IDF reserves the DQS and D4 to D7 pads (`spi_flash/flash_ops.c`, `esp_mspi_pin_reserve`); the design leaves board-held pins to SO-102's board input, so I only note it.
