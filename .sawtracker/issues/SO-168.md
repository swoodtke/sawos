**Networking K2: a second device window on arm64.**

Design: `SO:virtio-net` rev 4, §3.3 and §3.8.

- **The window.** arm64's per-process translation offers device pages only in the console's 2 MiB block, so mapping a virtio transport at 0x0A00_0000 faults. K2 adds a second device level-3 table over the transports' block.
- **The board.** The arm64 virtio-mmio rows, one per page of eight transports.
- **The runner and cases.** The arm64 net path and the probe cases on arm64.
