## Description

## Gap (from the review of SO-84's spec, Nov 13)

SO-84 lets the loader use a segment in place when it is already where it
is linked, and builds that for the ESP32-S3. The ESP32-C3 is the smaller
of the two boards, and its processes are still copied into SRAM: the rule
holds there, but nothing in its build links a segment where its image
sits. The user's scope for run-in-place was "these tiny devices where RAM
is limited".

## What is known

- The C3's kernel reads images in the flash instruction window
  (`hal/riscv32-esp32c3/kernel/esp32c3.ld`: `FLASH (rx)` at 0x4200_0000,
  `.payload` and `.childimg` there); its processes are linked in SRAM
  (`user/root.ld`, `user/child.ld`).
- `esp32c3.ld`'s own comment says a payload granted in place would not
  work there. Why, and whether that still holds, is the first thing to
  establish.
- Not established: whether the C3's buses serve byte loads in its flash
  windows, and whether its PMP can grant a process a range of flash. The
  board has never run on a part; the emulator is the only evidence.

## Before any work

- A C3 board, or a decision to build on the emulator's evidence alone.
- SO-84 merged: the loader's rule and the build's in-place check exist.
