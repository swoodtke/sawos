**ESP32-S3: a build key for the console's baud rate, so a fast image isn't bound by the console's wire.**

At the faster flash keys, `kill_churn` is console-bound. It prints 31,554 bytes from `kernel up` to `200 rounds`, which takes 2.739 s at 115200 8N1; the part's capture stamped the span at 2.753 s. The profile's console stall is 51–59% of `all`. The rate is the mask ROM's, and nothing in our images changes it.

**The owner's choice (Nov 25):** a build key for the console's baud. A buffered, timestamped log dumped at the end was weighed and left for later. It loses everything on a hang, and its RAM cost is large on the S3.

**To build:**
- **A keyed module**, as the clock and flash keys are: for example `s3console` with values `rom` (115200, the default, writing nothing) and faster rates.
  - The values depend on what the board's USB-serial bridge and the capture can open; the reviewer will tell us.
  - Name the values by rate.
- **The switch, early in boot:**
  - Wait for UART0 to drain (FIFO empty and transmitter idle), then write UART_CLKDIV for the new rate from the crystal clock UART0 is on (ABI.md's UART0 notes). Leave SCLK_SEL alone.
  - Say where it happens relative to the clock line, and print a line at the new rate saying the rate.
  - The ROM's own output before the switch stays at 115200.
- **Products and images can choose it** (`[keys] console = ...`), with the board's `[keys.console]` listing the values.
- **The runner's emulator and the product phase.** QEMU's serial isn't rate-limited, so the key must not change the transcript.
- **The `flash` command's output** tells whoever captures the image which rate to open.
- **Cases and refusals as for the other keys:**
  - an unknown value is refused;
  - a key stated twice is refused;
  - the stores are held exactly in a case;
  - a mutant for each check.
- **A board run:** `kill_churn_profile` at pll240/dio80 with the fastest working rate, against 115200. The console stall share should fall.

The design is small enough for one brief. Check ESP-IDF's UART rate setup on the S3 (`uart_ll_set_baudrate`, and the fractional divider in UART_CLKDIV) before writing the divider.
