**ESP32-S3: a build key for the console's baud rate, so a fast image isn't bound by the console's wire.**

At the faster flash keys, `kill_churn` is console-bound. It prints 31,554 bytes from `kernel up` to `200 rounds`, which takes 2.739 s at 115200 8N1; the part's capture stamped the span at 2.753 s. The profile's console stall is 51–59% of `all`. The rate is the mask ROM's, and nothing in our images changes it.

**The owner's choice (Nov 25):** a build key for the console's baud. A buffered, timestamped log dumped at the end was weighed and left for later. It loses everything on a hang, and its RAM cost is large on the S3.

**To build:**
- **A keyed module**, as the clock and flash keys are: for example `s3console` with values `rom` (115200, the default, writing nothing) and faster rates.
  - The values depend on what the board's USB-serial bridge and the capture can open; the reviewer will tell us.
  - Name the values by rate.
- **The switch, early in boot:**
  - Wait for UART0 to drain (FIFO empty and transmitter idle), then write UART_CLKDIV for the new rate from the crystal clock UART0 is on (ABI.md's UART0 notes). Leave SCLK_SEL alone.
  - Say where it happens relative to the clock line, and print a line at the new rate saying the rate.
  - The ROM's own output before the switch stays at 115200.
- **Products and images can choose it** (`[keys] console = ...`), with the board's `[keys.console]` listing the values.
- **The runner's emulator and the product phase.** QEMU's serial isn't rate-limited, so the key must not change the transcript.
- **The `flash` command's output** tells whoever captures the image which rate to open.
- **Cases and refusals as for the other keys:**
  - an unknown value is refused;
  - a key stated twice is refused;
  - the stores are held exactly in a case;
  - a mutant for each check.
- **A board run:** `kill_churn_profile` at pll240/dio80 with the fastest working rate, against 115200. The console stall share should fall.

The design is small enough for one brief. Check ESP-IDF's UART rate setup on the S3 (`uart_ll_set_baudrate`, and the fractional divider in UART_CLKDIV) before writing the divider.

Requested changes on patch SO-161.p1 (revision 2)

**Changes requested, revision 2,** for one unchecked field. Everything else holds. r2 is r1 with comments rewrapped. I ran r2 on the S3 smoke with sawlang v0.24.3, against `console_found_*` and `console_line_*` at the three rates (6 green).

**What holds:**
- **The divisor** is ESP-IDF v5.4.1's `uart_ll_set_baudrate` for a 40 MHz clock with no pre-divide: the quotient of 16 × 40 MHz over the baud, its low four bits at [23:20] and the rest at [11:0]. That gives 0x0060_002B, 0x0000_0014 and 0x0050_000D, as the cases hold.
- **The registers** match `uart_reg.h`:
  - CLKDIV at +0x14, with CLKDIV [11:0] and FRAG [23:20];
  - STATUS at +0x1C, with TXFIFO_CNT [25:16];
  - FSM_STATUS at +0x6C, with ST_UTX_OUT [7:4];
  - CLK_CONF at +0x78, with SCLK_SEL [21:20] (3 is the crystal);
  - the base 0x6000_0000.
- **Red mutants:**
  - the select check dropped, on all six cases;
  - the drain dropped, or moved after the store, on `console_found_*`;
  - FRAG at [19:16], and the quotient rounded up, on 921600 and 3000000.
- **The builder:** `console_rate` maps each value to its rate and refuses the rest.

## 1. A crystal clock with a pre-divide is taken
The switch checks SCLK_SEL alone. The divisor assumes SCLK_DIV_NUM, DIV_A and DIV_B are 0, "as the ROM leaves it". But `uart_reg.h` gives SCLK_DIV_NUM a reset value of 1, and nothing reads the field.

**The probe** (f565): the harness also hands the switch 0x0370_1000, which is the crystal with SCLK_DIV_NUM = 1. The switch takes it: `SOS-S3: console b921600 clkdiv=0x0060002b->0x0060002b uart_clk=0x03701000`. Under that word the crystal is halved, so the console would run at 460,800 while the line, and the `flash` command's rate line, say 921,600.

The board's ROM does leave the field 0 (`uart_clk=0x03700000`). But the select check exists for words the ROM is not expected to leave, and this is the same kind of word.

**Asked:** refuse ("not taken") unless SCLK_DIV_NUM, DIV_A and DIV_B are all 0, with 0x0370_1000 (or a DIV_A/DIV_B word) as a refused word in `console_found`. Or compute the divisor through the pre-divide, as IDF does.

## 2. The drain's transmitter-idle half has no case
`if fifo == 0` alone, with ST_UTX_OUT dropped, survives all six cases: under the emulator the FSM reads idle. The board run's byte-perfect output at each rate is evidence that it works, not a case.

**Asked:** a lie on the FSM_STATUS read in the harness, as `flash_switch` lies to its reads, or name it in the case's comment as a board-only check.
