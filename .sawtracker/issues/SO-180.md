# Networking A4: a DHCP client and a DNS stub, against the network peer

The networking design is `SO:networking`, its §6.1 and §10's track A. The
stack is written in Saw, UDP first. This unit is **A4**.

A1–A3 have merged:
- `drivers/netstack` is the stack's first layer (Ethernet, ARP, IPv4, ICMP,
  UDP) over byte buffers, with hosted tests;
- `tools/netpeer` is the peer a `net` case boots beside QEMU, with scripted
  behaviours;
- `drivers/svc-virtio-net` is the driver;
- `tests/net-ping` and `tests/net-client` are the clients.

## Read first

- `drivers/netstack/src/*.saw` and its tests: the shape of a layer, with no
  device and no clock of its own.
- `tools/netpeer/src/peer.saw` and its scripts.
- The `net_*` cases in `tools/sos_runner.py`, and CLAUDE.md's `net` section.
- `tests/net-ping`: how a client drives the stack through the driver.
- RFC 2131 (DHCP) and RFC 2132 (its options), and RFC 1035 (DNS). Use what a
  small client needs; cite sections in comments only where a rule isn't
  obvious.

## Build

1. **DHCP in `drivers/netstack`**, as a layer over UDP:
   - the client side of DISCOVER, OFFER, REQUEST and ACK, with NAK
     handled;
   - the options a client needs: message type, server id, requested
     address, lease time, subnet mask, router, DNS server;
   - a state machine with no clock of its own. Retransmission and lease
     timing are driven by the caller giving it the time, as netstack's
     other layers are.

   Hosted tests:
   - golden packets each way;
   - every refusal: wrong xid, malformed options, a truncated packet, an
     OFFER for another client;
   - the state transitions.
2. **A DNS stub in `drivers/netstack`:**
   - an A-record query and the response parser, with name compression;
   - the refusals: a mismatched id, a truncated or malformed answer, a
     compression loop, a name past its limits;
   - hosted golden tests.
3. **The peer** (`tools/netpeer`) gains a DHCP server script and a DNS
   answer script, with hosted tests. Keep them small and deterministic:
   one lease, and a fixed name table.
4. **Gate cases**, on riscv32, riscv32-flat and arm64:
   - `net_dhcp`: the client gets a lease from the peer and prints the
     address, router and DNS server;
   - `net_dns`: it resolves a name the peer knows, and one it doesn't
     (NXDOMAIN).

   Extend `tests/net-ping` or `tests/net-client` by a build-input scenario,
   whichever fits.
   - Order every line by construction, never by time.
   - Retransmission needs a clock. A case may show that a lost DISCOVER is
     retried (the peer drops the first one), asserting only that a second
     DISCOVER arrived, never when it did.
5. **Mutants:** every check needs a test or case that goes red when it's
   removed or inverted. Name each one in your note.

**SO-180.p1 note (A4).**
**Stack:**
- Unaddressed, the stack takes and sends only UDP to and from 255.255.255.255, sending from 0.0.0.0; everything else is a counted drop.
- `Stack.deconfigure()` clears the address, drops the frames waiting on ARP and forgets the ARP table.

**`dhcp.saw`:**
- 300-byte BOOTP messages.
- `parse_reply` refuses: Truncated, NotReply, BadHardware, WrongXid, NotOurs, BadCookie, BadOption, BadLength, MissingType, BadType, MissingOption, BadLease and BadConfig. The client also refuses Unexpected, WrongServer and WrongAddress.
- States: Init, Selecting, Requesting, Bound, Renewing and Rebinding, driven by the caller's `now`.
  - A DISCOVER retries with doubling to a cap; a REQUEST is tried 5 times.
  - T1 is 1/2 and T2 is 7/8: renewal by unicast, rebinding by broadcast.
  - A NAK or the lease's end gives `Lost`, and an infinite lease never renews.

**`dns.saw`:**
- An A query; name refusals (Empty, EmptyLabel, LabelTooLong, TooLong, BadCharacter) and response refusals (Truncated, WrongId, NotResponse, BadOpcode, Cut, BadQuestion, WrongQuestion, BadLabel, BadPointer, NameTooLong, BadRecord, BadAddress).
- Compression pointers lead strictly backward, at most 16 jumps.
- CNAME chains are followed, names compare case-insensitively, and the answers are Found, NoSuchName, NoData or Failed.

**Peer:** scripts `dhcp` (10.0.2.15/24, router .2, DNS .3, 3600 s), `dhcp_retry` (drops the first DISCOVER) and `dns` (`sawos.test`, an alias with compression, NXDOMAIN otherwise).

**Cases** (three profiles):
- `net_dhcp`: lease, configure, ping the router;
- `net_dhcp_retry`: asserts only that a second DISCOVER arrived; the clock stops being read after it;
- `net_dns`.

**Mutants:** 137 hosted (dhcp 54, dns 36, lib 10, peer 36), all red, plus four gate mutants red. Seven first survived: three tests were added, and four dead checks removed.

**Totals:** gate 1650, S3 519.

**Lock:** the run printed a stale-owner clear (a dead pid), and two suites may have overlapped briefly. The owner pid in agents' scripts is sometimes a short-lived shell. Future briefs require one script that takes the lock, writes its own `$$`, runs the command and releases, all in one process.
