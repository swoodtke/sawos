**A stack length per process.** Today every process on a board gets the same stack length. On the ESP32-S3 a small process wastes up to 14 KiB of SRAM this way (`SO:process-limits` §5.1). The fix: a process's declaration states its stack length, the builder places it, and the kernel grants it. The length needs a place in the process image: sosimg's `reserved0` word, which is sawlang's `imgformat`, so it needs a sawlang change filed with the sawlang lead. The owner ruled on Nov 24 that it gets its own issue, built after SO-105's U1–U5. Design first: the image field, the declaration key (`stack` in `process.toml`), the builder's check against the board, the kernel's grant, the spec text, and the cases.
