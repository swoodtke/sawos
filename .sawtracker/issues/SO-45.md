## Description

These are follow-ups from the SO-31 r7 approval (claude-air-reviewer). Line numbers are r6's.

1. **§11, the SO-41 line (around 2700-2704).**
   - Add that a caller error through another process's handle faults that process. Cite the fault-target issue.
   - `PipeCreate` hands back two words.
   - "child-relative" is narrower than "another process's handle".
2. **The SO-39 marker (1596-1599 and 2705-2707).** It could name the kernel's ambiguous `PeerClosed` for `send_with`, not only the carrier ordering.
3. **"(§2.1, §3)" at 2707.** §2.1 states only the reply half.
4. **2840-2841.** "with nothing given, is the sandboxed compute process … and is the default" reads as if giving nothing were the default. "Default" belongs to the no-tag form.
5. **§2.7 (around 1042).** The list of factory ops is missing `ThreadCreate`.

This is a small spec-only patch, to be done after the cleanup epic lands.

**Lead review, SO-45.p1** (spec.md only; rebased onto current main).

**Items**
1. **§11, the factory-ops line.**
   - It now says the caller is handed words relative to the named process (two for `PipeCreate`).
   - A caller error through another process's handle faults the named process: `ThreadCreate` with `BadEntry`, `EventCreate` with `BadArg`, `InterruptBind` with `BadArg`/`LineBound`.
   - The caller's running thread is then never scheduled again. `end_process` sets `CURRENT_THREAD = NO_THREAD` and states that it assumes the dying process is the caller.
   - `PipeCreate` faults the caller, and `WaiterCreate` has no fault path.
2. **§11, new line:** `BootHandleNext` and `ThreadSelf` check the right on the handle but act on the caller.
3. **§3 and §11, the `send_with` marker** now names the cause. The kernel answers `PeerClosed` both before staging (`pipe_call`, `resume_call_room`) and after it (`wake_call_reply`). `call_body` disarms first, so the typed tier can't tell whether the handles came back.
4. **The §11 pointer** is now "(§3; §2.1 states the reply half)".
5. **§12:** the no-tag form is the default.
6. **§2.7:** the ops list matches all 12 `ProcessOp` cases.
7. **§2.5:** `blade/src/sosimg.saw`.

**Also added:** a §5.7 marker. The uniform "terminates the offending process" rule is not yet enforced through another process's handle for those three ops (§11).

**Checks**
- `md_facts --spec`: the only dropped span is `blade/sosimg.saw`. No hex constants or headings were dropped. No new unresolved §: §3a, §7a and §7b are the C3 ABI.md's own sections and are listed on main too.
- No added line is over 80 columns.

**Air: SO-45.p1, approved.** Each item holds against the code:
- **§2.7's ops list** names all 12 `ProcessOp` cases (`ThreadCreate` through `Stats`).
- **§11's factory-ops line:**
  - words relative to the named process, two for `PipeCreate`;
  - the `BadEntry`/`BadArg`/`LineBound` faults on the named process, with the stranded caller from `end_process`'s `CURRENT_THREAD = NO_THREAD`;
  - `PipeCreate` faulting the caller, and `WaiterCreate` with no fault path.
  
  §5.7's new marker points at it.
- **The `BootHandleNext`/`ThreadSelf` line** matches `boot_handle_next(p, …)` and `CURRENT_THREAD`.
- **The `send_with` cause (§3, §11):** `pipe_call` answers `PeerClosed` on `outlet_refs == 0` before staging, `resume_call_room` writes it at the room-wait resume, and `wake_call_reply` defaults to it after staging. So the status can't say whether the handles were taken.
- **The rest:** the §11 pointer "(§3; §2.1 states the reply half)", §12's no-tag wording (the form is the default) and `blade/src/sosimg.saw`.
