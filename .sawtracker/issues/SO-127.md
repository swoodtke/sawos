## Description

**Unit B of the PSRAM design (SO-102, doc `SO:s3-psram` revision 3): the command layer.** Unit A (SO-109, the read-only probe) is merged and the part has run it (capture on SO-109: PSRAM_CAP 1, PSRAM_VENDOR 1, 8 MiB AP at 3.3 V; the ROM's dummy bytes 0). The owner answered yes to every one of the design's questions.

**Build what the design's §10 lists for unit B**, under §2.2's rules: `hal/xtensa-esp32s3/kernel/mspi.c` with `sos_psram_bringup` (S0 to S10 and `last_step`), S0.3's full clock compare, the record, the bounded `wait_idle`, the IRAM short line, the register accessor with its `SOS_PSRAM_FAULT` variant, and `sos_psram_time_reads` for the board's P2; `esp32s3.ld`'s IRAM gains `*(.literal.iram .text.iram)`; the runner's `check_iram` with its five mutations as mutation checks, and its refusal of `sos_psram_fault` outside harness images; harness kernels `psram_identity`, `psram_mode` and §6.7's fault harness; the runner's arch key for extra QEMU arguments (`-m 8M -global ssi_psram.is_octal=true`), a per-run eFuse file, and the trace assertion that the flash model is selected exactly once; smoke cases for the identity lines, every stop 0x50 to 0x5D that §6.7 reaches (each with its SPI1-restored assertion), the trace case (red with the gate removed), and 0x59's case (red with S10 run on 0x59).

**Two things the design does not yet say, part of this unit:**
1. **The reviewer's thread t9 (accepted):** the timing measurement's stage reads 2 and 10 bytes, not 64, so it stays inside the RAM chip's chip-select-low limit at 5 MHz. Write it so in `sos_psram_time_reads` and in ABI.md.
2. **The PLL is now built (SO-113):** the `pll80` clock key moves the CPU to the PLL, which also moves the memory-SPI core clock (80 MHz at 480 MHz, CORE_CLK_SEL 0; ABI.md §14d, and the board run on SO-113 read `mspi_core=0x00000000 mspi_clock=0x00030103`). The design's §3 assumed the crystal's clock with three possible readings of the core. State in ABI.md what the core clock is under each clock key (`rom`, `xtal40`, `pll80`), and make S0.3's clock compare and the early stages' divider correct under each key the S3 smoke and the board use, or refuse a key it cannot serve, by name. If this changes a design rule rather than filling one in, stop and send a `CLARIFICATION REQUEST:`.

**Also first:** fill ABI.md §15's table from the part's capture on SO-109 (the memory-SPI modes and the pads the probe read), before the bring-up relies on them.

**Not in this unit:** S11 onward (the map, the ranges, the `esp32s3-r8` board: unit C); a process in PSRAM (unit D); any board run (stages P1 and P2 are run by the reviewer after merge, on the lead's request).

**The review standard (the owner's ruling, Nov 15):** a patch with a gap the reviewer can name is held for revision. Every check and stop has a case that goes red when it is removed or inverted; list the mutants.

Requested changes on patch SO-127.p1 (revision 1)

**Changes requested. Nothing here can reach the flash, leave it deselected by accident, or write anything a power cycle does not undo; I looked for that first and found the patch sound. But by the capture this patch files in §15c, two of the octal bus's eight data lines are not on the controller, and four checks have no case.** Evidence: chat f179. The server's run passed; all 95 S3 cases pass here.

## 1. Pads 27 and 28: D3 and D2 of the bus are still GPIOs on this part

- **The part:** §15c's cells, from the capture: `26=0x00001b00 27=0x00001b00 28=0x00001b00 29=0x00000b00 30 31 32=0x00000b00`. MCU_SEL [14:12] is 1 on 27 and 28, and function 1 there is the GPIO (`FUNC_SPIHD_GPIO27` 1, `FUNC_SPIWP_GPIO28` 1; `FUNC_SPIHD_SPIHD` and `FUNC_SPIWP_SPIWP` are 0; io_mux_reg.h). The flash is read on one line, so the ROM never gave the controller HD and WP.
- **ESP-IDF:** `esp_mspi_pin_init` (spi_flash/flash_ops.c) calls `esp_rom_opiflash_pin_config()` whenever the PSRAM is octal. The ROM's table at 0x3FF1_90A8 is pads `30 29 32 31 28 27 33 34 35 36 37`, with functions `0 0 0 0 0 0 4 4 4 4 4` at 0x3FF1_90B3 and input signals `-1 -1 1 0 3 2 7 8 9 10 11`: it puts 27 and 28 at function 0 and bypasses signals 2 and 3 with the rest.
- **The patch:** "S1: pad 26, then pads 33 to 37, then the input bypass. Pads 27 to 32, the flash's, are never written" (`mspi.c`); §15c: "the flash's own pads (27 to 32) are the ROM's and S1 does not touch them". The design's row for them says "dropped: the flash's pads, which `SelectSpiFunction` has already set for the running flash". On this part it has not set two of them.
- **What follows:** in octal DTR every command and data byte is on D0 to D7. With D2 and D3 pulled up and undriven I expect the chip not to see `0x40`, and stage P1 to stop at 0x5A or 0x5B. That expectation is mine, not measured; the emulator's bus is byte-serial and cannot show it.
- **It is a design question before it is a code one:** the fix writes the running flash's WP# and HOLD# pads, which the design's §8.1 counts among the ways to lose the part. As the patch stands those two pads are untouched and stay pulled high, so nothing here is unsafe; it would only not work.

## 2. Four checks with no case (each mutant leaves all 34 PSRAM cases green)

- **A held data pad.** `PSRAM_PADS_HELD` cut down to pad 26's bit alone: green. `psram_stop_pad_held` injects `0x20`, pad 26; nothing injects a bit of 12 to 16.
- **The entry check's top.** `addr <= DRAM_HIGH - len` made `addr < DRAM_HIGH`: green. The three 0x50 cases put the description or the record in flash; none puts one across the end of DRAM.
- **The restore of CLOCK(1).** S10 leaving that one word unwritten: green, where each of the other ten turns between 2 and 12 cases red. The description's divider is the word already there, so the comparison cannot tell. The design's S10 names it ("`CLOCK(1)` among them"); a case with another divider would hold it.
- **The timing reads' length.** The brief: "the timing measurement's stage reads 2 and 10 bytes, not 64 ... Write it so in `sos_psram_time_reads` and in ABI.md." §16b has it. The entry's comment says "(1 to 64, what W0 to W15 hold)" and the code takes `bytes` as it comes: 0, 64 or 1000 reaches `MISO_DLEN` unchecked, and 64 is what the brief rules out at 5 MHz. Bound it there with a stop, or have the entry take no length at all.

## 3. Smaller

- **A refused board image stays on disk.** `build_flash` links and writes the image before `check_iram` and `check_psram_fault` run, so `psram_fault_board_image` is "refused" with its `.board.bin` left in the build directory. Remove it on a refusal.
- **0x57 restores SPI1 while `SPI_MEM_CMD(1)` still reads busy; 0x59 does not, "so a transfer still under way cannot be turned towards the flash by a restore".** The design marks 0x57 "(S10 first)", so the code is the design's. No command of ours is under way there (the wait before it read 0). Say why the two differ, in one sentence where 0x57 is described.
- **§15c:** "`user1` `0x5c000007` (a 24-bit address, 8 dummy cycles)" beside `user` `0x000000c0`: USR_DUMMY, bit 29, is clear, so no dummy is sent whatever the field holds. And "`sctrl` `0x0055c070` ... nothing set for the PSRAM's side": that word is the reset default, with several of the PSRAM's read bits set.
- **§16b:** "`MSPI_TIMING_LL_FLASH_CPU_CLK_SRC_BINDED` to 1, which says the memory-SPI clock's source follows the CPU's". At v5.4.1 that is a bare define with no comment and no user; the reading is from its name.

## What holds

- **The gate.** Every command is sent with `MISC(1)` [1:0] = 01, re-read before each start bit: in `command` the check stands before the one store to `CMD`, and `saved_offsets` ends with `MISC`, so it is the last word put back (an agent of mine found the same order in the compiled object). With the gate value made 2 (the flash selected) or 0, eleven and thirteen cases turn red; with either check removed, its stop's case does.
- **Every address `mspi.c` can write**, by reading all of it: IO MUX 26 and 33 to 37, the five input selects, SPI0's `SMEM_AC` and `SRAM_CLK`, SYSCON's ECC bit only when it reads set, twelve SPI1 words, `CMD(1)` only ever `0x00040000`, UART0's FIFO. The eFuse word, the RTC hold word and the three clock words are read only. The commands are `0x4040`, `0xC0C0` and `0x0000`.
- **Each step's register, bits and order** against §16c, the design, ESP-IDF v5.4.1 and the ROM, step by step by that agent; I re-read item 1's lines, the ROM's pin tables, and both things your note asks: the ROM's mode-7 entry (0x4004_DD2F) ORs `0x2270` into `CTRL`, then at offset 224 clears bit 4 (`DDR_CMD_DIS`) and sets bit 0 (`DDR_EN`); and `psram_stop_gate_lost` asserts `starts=0x00000000 w0_reads=0x00000000` together.
- **Mutants: 44 of `mspi.c`, 31 red** (the gate's four, the restore's presence on each path, every wait and the short line, both eFuse fields, the three clock words, ECC, vendor, die, density, MR0's read-back and value, S9 running at the identity stage, the entry check's two addresses and its bottom, the two command words). **Nine more survive as the design's §6.6 says they must on this emulator**, and are P1's and P2's to judge: the pads' two functions and the input bypass, the octal bits, `DDR_EN`, the dummy length, both dividers, the chip-select timing. "MISC last" also has no case, and I see no way the emulator could give one.
- **Every stop 0x50 to 0x5D** has its case, its §5.2 line and, but for 0x59, the SPI1-restored line.

**Four survivors, each harmless, for the record:**

- the length test loosened to "at most 10": lengths 0 to 9 send no longer a read than the long one, and a zero length sends no data phase (`in_bits != 0`);
- the pad mask without 34 to 36: both ends and pad 26 are held, the middle three are one literal's bits;

**Item 1, for the owner's ruling.** What I can add to it: the capture has pads 27 and 28 at `0x00001b00`, function 1 with the pull-up on. That is why I expect P1 to stop at its identity check. It is a reading, not a measurement; the board has not been written for this.
