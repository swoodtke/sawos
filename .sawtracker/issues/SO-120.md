## Description

**Unit U5 of the image and packaging design (SO-95): the quota door.** A launcher sets a child's quota rows between its create and its start, and any holder of `Stats` reads a process's limit and use. The user approved the spec on Nov 14 (SO-106: SO-106.p1 and p2 merged); this unit builds every sentence marked `(not yet enforced: quota-door)` and removes the markers.

**What is built.** `spec.md` is authoritative: §2.7 "Quota rows" (the op, the right, a row as a cap on one process and not a budget shared with the launcher, the bound, and the answers in the order they are decided: the dispatch's; the kind; the process's state; the limit's faults; then the machine's statuses, a `Mapping` limit past the child's free grant rows and a `Handle` limit past `MAX_HANDLES`, each `NoResource`); `ProcessOp.QuotaGet(kind)` under `Stats` answering the limit and the use in one word; §2.9 (a child's rows: the image's defaults, then whatever its launcher sets before the start); §2.10's table (`QuotaSet` through a `Gone` child's handle is `BadState`, as `Start` and `Give` are); and every other marked sentence (find them all: `grep -n "quota-door" spec.md`).
- **The ABI.** `QuotaKind` and the unlimited sentinel are kernel-internal today (`kernel/core/limits.saw`); the door needs both published: the kinds' numbers in `kernel/abi` beside the other op numbers, and the public `sos` module (`kernel/sysapi`) gains `Process.quota_set(kind, limit)` and `Process.quota_get(kind)` with a type for the answer. Follow how the existing ops and rights are spelled and published (one place for every number, vDSO discipline: numbers are not the userspace ABI).
- **The kernel.** The op in the dispatch, its right in the process rights and in the rights the create mints, the checks in the spec's order, and the ledger write. A child's row is already set at its create from the image's defaults or the compiled ones (`install_quota_limits`, `child_quota_default`); the door changes it before the start and only then.

**Cases**, on every gate profile:
- each answer of the spec's order, one case per answer, each failure line not containing its expected line: the right missing; an unknown kind; a started child, and a `Gone` one; the sentinel; a limit above the caller's own for a kind but `Mapping`; a limit below what the child already holds (for a kind a created child can already be charged: threads, events, Waiters, pipes or mappings made through its handle); a `Mapping` limit past the child's free rows and a `Handle` limit past `MAX_HANDLES` (each `NoResource`, nothing set);
- a limit set and then held: a child given a lower limit than its default is refused one past it, and one given a higher limit (within the caller's own) makes more than its default;
- `QuotaGet` on itself and on a child, limit and use both, before and after a use;
- the order where two answers overlap (a limit above both the caller's own and the table is the fault, since the limit's faults come before the machine's answer);
- each new check shown to hold its line by a one-operator mutant applied alone, red then green with the source restored byte for byte.

**Not in this unit:** per-process limits beyond the door (SO-105); lowering a started process's limits; the per-board values (U2b).
