## Description

## Finding

From the SO-26 r2 rewrite of the rights docs. The documented way for a launcher to start a child without some Process right (say `InterruptBind`) is: withhold `SystemRight.ProcessSelf` from the child's System handle, and **give** the child a Process handle narrowed by `ProcessOp.Give`'s keep mask. That works at the ABI and in the C floor (`boot_kind_of` accepts Process), but **sysapi has no typed `Process.give(process:…)`**, and `Process`'s handle word is private. A Saw launcher can't do it today. (The `give(process:key:)` in `system.saw` is `Waiter.give`, an attach, not a transfer.)

## Proposed

Add `Process.give(process: Process, tag:, keep:)`, mirroring the other `give` overloads: disarm before the syscall, with a hand-back on a status refusal once SO-38's shape lands. Also `give(process:)` on the pipe message path, if Process is to travel in messages (today `msg_kind_of` refuses Process and System at the sender, by design).

Low priority, an API gap rather than a bug. It needs a ruling on whether Process handles should be givable through the typed tier at all.
