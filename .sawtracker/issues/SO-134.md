## Description

**Unit U2 of the GPIO/PWM drivers design (SO-77): the GPIO driver.** U1 (SO-131) has merged. The owner accepted every recommendation of the design's §11 (Nov 16); the useless box (SO-78) reads its switch through this driver.

**Build what §12's U2 lists, as U1 left the ground** (read scratchpad `SO-77/u1-as-built.md` first: where it and the design differ, it wins): `drivers/gpioproto/` (§4's protocol), the GPIO driver package and its `process.toml`, its configurations, the smoke cases `gpio_service` (a described S3 image: the stock root, the driver, a test client child) and `gpio_bad_config` (runner cases on the stock root with `root_plan`, `devices`, `inputs`), and the board cases G1 (nothing wired) and G2 (a switch from GPIO 21 to ground, flipped ten times by the owner). Built on `esp32s3io` (`start_gpio`, `service_interrupt`, the config checks); pins 19 and 20 and the flash/console pads are refused there already.

**Red cases:** each of §8.4's GPIO rows, as amended by `u1-as-built.md`; the patch note lists the mutants run, one per row and more where a check has several bounds. The review standard (the owner's ruling, Nov 15): every check, refusal and stop has a case or test that goes red when it is removed or inverted.

**Board:** the lead requests G1 and G2 through the reviewer under the owner's standing approval; build the images (`--emit-flash`) with expected text and device-write lists. G2 needs the owner's switch wired; G1 needs nothing.

**Not in this unit:** the PWM driver (U3), the box (SO-78).
