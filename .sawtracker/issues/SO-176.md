## Build

1. **`tests/net-client`.** One test package whose scenario is a build input
   (`netcase`), given per image by its `inputs`, as `tests/net-ping`'s
   `pingconfig` is. Each scenario attaches, does its one hostile or edge
   step, prints what it sees, and ends by itself. It exits nonzero on any
   failure path, so no case reaches the harness timeout.
2. **The rest of §7.3's table,** on riscv32, riscv32-flat and arm64, each
   with its `tests/images/net_*.toml`:
   - `net_link_down_first`, `net_tx_length`;
   - `net_ring_index_past`, `net_ring_index_order`, `net_ring_length_past`,
     `net_ring_tail_past`;
   - `net_attach_too_large`, `net_attach_handle`, `net_ring_no_physical`,
     `net_protocol_states`;
   - `net_slow_client`, `net_client_dies`, `net_wait_abandoned`.

   Leave out any row A3-4 already covers, and say which.
3. **`net_attach_handle`.** The design's request carries "an inlet sibling
   of its own". Root gives every client inlet `Post | Transfer` with no
   `Mint`, and carrying the inlet being called through is `BadArg`. So
   carry something else, as the reviewer suggested: a `Memory` cut from the
   client's heap pool. `HEAP_POOL_KEEP` includes `Transfer`.
4. **The two cases A3-4's review handed here:**
   - **`Attach` answering `NoResource`** (the driver reports it as the status
     `NoMemory` and keeps the connection). Give an image `[limits]` that
     leaves the Memory slab exactly full at the ring's allocation, beside
     `net_attach_too_large`. If the builder or kernel makes that exact fit
     impossible to construct, say why and don't force it.
   - **Receive buffers posted at `Attach`** (mutant M07). Write a peer
     script that speaks first, and a client scenario that only listens.
     QEMU queues the frame until a buffer is posted, so a driver that posts
     none at `Attach` leaves the client hanging. Expected: the client
     receives the frame.
5. **The peer's scripts.** `burst` and any others §7.3 needs, plus the
   speak-first script, in `tools/netpeer` with its hosted tests.

## Ordering and time

- No case asserts host time (CLAUDE.md). Every ordering is forced by
  construction: by a wait on the other side's action, never a delay.
  `net_wait_abandoned`'s yield ordering relies on the image arming no tick,
  as §7.3 explains. Check that this still holds under the priority
  scheduler's levels: a yield at one level, and a wake that preempts only a
  lower level.
- §7.3's paragraph on the hostile cases' end order ("the client's
  `PeerClosed` line before root stops") is stale. Root stops once every
  child has ended. Leave an unforced line out of the expected text and say
  so, as A3-4's note did.
