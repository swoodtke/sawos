## Description

Two small pre-existing doc inaccuracies, found during SO-36:

1. **`README.md`** says a sawlang checkout is needed because "the runtime and image-format packages are compiled from source". The runtime (`sosrt`) is in-tree (`rt/common/`). Only `imgformat`, and Blade's sources when no `blade` binary is supplied, come from the checkout.
2. **`tools/sos_runner.py`, around line 2479:** "unmap's revocation half is the other property Flat disclaims". Under the flat-tier ruling, the flat tier disclaims protecting memory from a process, the kernel's included, so "the other property" undercounts. Restate it as one aspect of that disclaimer.
