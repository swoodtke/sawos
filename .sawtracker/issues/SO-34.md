## Description

## Scope

After the v0.20.0 pin, sawc supplies `mem*`, the `__atomic_*` libcalls and the 64-bit division helpers in Saw (sawlang SL-945/SL-948). `rt/common_c/support.c` still defines them, and its strong C `memcpy` overrides std's weak one at the ld.lld link.

The bump survey compiled those definitions out (`#if 0`): all 382 cases passed on three profiles. Only the two arena accessors (`sos_arena_base`/`sos_arena_size`) are still needed, because Saw cannot name a linker symbol's address (SL-136).

## Proposed change

Delete the redundant definitions from `support.c`, keep the arena accessors, and update its header comment. Not yet verified: the ESP32-C3 smoke (no-A multilib), and whether any image size moves.

**Lead review, SO-34.p1** (rebased onto current main).

**Scope as ruled.** `support.c` keeps the arena accessors and the four `__atomic_*_4` functions, and drops `mem*` and the 64-bit division helpers. Deleting the atomics broke the ESP32-C3 link (undefined `__atomic_load_4`/`fetch_add_4`/`fetch_sub_4` from std's `String`). That is by design: sawlang's LANGUAGE_SPEC Profiles section puts the word atomics on the environment for riscv32 without `+a`, and SL-948 c4's revised ruling says std supplies none. The fifth environment atomic, `__atomic_compare_exchange_4`, is missing and filed as SO-47.

**Evidence**
- **Symbols (`llvm-nm`):** `mem*` and `__udivdi3` are std's weak definitions, defined once. The atomics are defined only on the C3, from `support.c`. Nothing is undefined.
- **SO-4 (`llvm-objdump -d`):** the arm64 kernel ELF and the arm64 user images, Saw and `c-child`, have no q/d/s/v register or `f*` instruction. The kernel's IRQ-path `memcpy` is now std's integer-only one, so a trap doesn't clobber EL0's vector registers. `boot.S` and arm64 `ABI.md` now state that. The frame still saves no FP/SIMD state, and the C build doesn't forbid vector code, so `ABI.md` keeps "(open: SO-4)" for the remaining half.
- **Runs:**
  - Ten targeted cases pass on three profiles.
  - The C3 smoke passes 3/3, with flash unchanged at 88664/100952/105304.
  - `c_hello`, `thread_basics` and `thread_preempt` pass on arm64.
- **Sizes:** sosimg sizes are unchanged except `svc-client`, +16 on riscv32/flat.

**Docs**
- `spec.md` §5c: the reasons for C are an instruction with no Saw spelling, and what only the environment can supply (linker-symbol addresses, the word atomics).
- The C3 `ABI.md`: the atomics are plain read-modify-write, sound in the kernel (interrupts masked) and in a user process only while nothing preempts it.
- CLAUDE.md's repo-map line and three runner comments are updated to match.

`comment_equiv` flags only the four .md files (prose) and `support.c` (the deliberate deletions). `boot.S` and `sos_runner.py` are comment-only.
