## Description

**The PLL on the ESP32-S3: 80 MHz first, built on the kernel's window support.** The user ruled on Nov 14: "build the windowed support now. then get the pll working."

SO-110 built the crystal step and listed, in `hal/xtensa-esp32s3/ABI.md` §14b with sources, what the PLL needs and why it stopped there. The board then measured 19.5 MHz as the ROM leaves it and 40.01 MHz at the crystal (SO-110's captures), with `kill_churn` twice as fast.

**What this unit builds, after the windowed-threads unit has landed:**
- a `pll80` clock key beside `rom` and `xtal40`: the BBPLL powered and configured as ESP-IDF v5.4.1's `rtc_clk_bbpll_enable` and `rtc_clk_bbpll_configure` do, its analog words written through the ROM's own `rom_i2c_writeReg` and `rom_i2c_writeReg_Mask` (windowed), called from the call0 kernel through a stub that turns the window machinery on, calls in and turns it off, with the kernel's real window handlers under it; then the switch to the PLL and the CPU period for 80 MHz, from IRAM;
- everything that moves with it, each handled or shown not to move: the APB clock, the console (UART0's clock is the crystal on this part), SYSTIMER, and the flash clock, whose divider must be valid at the new core clock before any instruction is fetched from flash again;
- the RTC_CNTL writes this needs beyond `_start`'s (the BBPLL's power bits) are allowed by the ruling; each is listed with its source, is volatile, and is undone by a reset;
- the clock line and the measured MHz as the other keys print them; `kill_churn` and `kill_churn_profile` images at `pll80` for the board.

**160 and 240 MHz are a second step, on the user's word after the 80 MHz numbers:** ESP-IDF raises the digital LDO voltage before running at those speeds, from calibration values read out of eFuse (reads only), and the lead asked the user to decide that with the first measurement in hand.

**The flash clock is the other half.** At 80 MHz the memory-SPI core clock can give the flash 20, 40 or 80 MHz. Say which this unit sets and why, and what ESP-IDF's timing tuning requires above which speed; a faster flash clock than the ROM's read mode supports is not taken without its source.

**Lead: SO-113.p1 is up: the PLL at 80 MHz, as the `pll80` clock key.** Built on SO-112.p2 and rebased by the lead onto main after it merged (`47371c9c`); its added and removed lines are the implementer's commit's, unchanged. Gate on the implementer's machine: 1034 / 6 / 61 and 1 board-only (`boot_pll80`, `kill_churn_pll80` and `pll_bringup` new). Board images are with the reviewer (chat f171); the part's captures decide what the emulator cannot.

**The bring-up** (`sos_pll_bringup`, `boot.S`, IRAM), run only when SYSCLK_CONF reads the crystal, each step cited to ESP-IDF v5.4.1 in ABI.md §14b:
- P1 `RTC_CNTL_OPTIONS0 &= ~0x540` (BB_I2C_FORCE_PD, BBPLL_I2C_FORCE_PD, BBPLL_FORCE_PD), the only RTC write;
- R3 `ANA_CONFIG |= 0x3FF << 8`, then bit 17 cleared (`regi2c_ctrl_ll_i2c_reset`, `_bbpll_enable`);
- P2 PLL_FREQ_SEL 480 in CPU_PER_CONF; P3 the calibration started in I2C_MST_ANA_CONF0;
- P4 the BBPLL's seven analog words through the mask ROM's `rom_i2c_writeReg` and `_Mask` (block 0x66), in `clk_ll_bbpll_set_config(480, 40)`'s order;
- P5 CAL_DONE polled for at most 4,000,000 cycles (ESP-IDF waits without a bound); P6 400 cycles' settling; P7 the calibration stopped, on either path.
Then the switch: CPU_PER_CONF's CPUPERIOD_SEL, SYSCLK_CONF's PRE_DIV_CNT 0 and SOC_CLK_SEL 1, each a store from IRAM. A calibration that never reports done prints a `not taken` line and leaves the CPU on the ROM's clock. Not written: SLAVE_PD (the ROM leaves all six LDO slaves on, the side that cannot droop), any LDO, voltage, eFuse, flash or memory-SPI register; the flash runs at 80 / 4 = 20 MHz, ESP-IDF's low-speed mode, whose divider word is the ROM's.

**The stub changes:** it moves to IRAM (`.iram.text.windowed_call`), because the bring-up must not fetch through the flash cache while the PLL moves (ESP-IDF keeps that code in IRAM "because disabling BBPLL may influence the cache") and it calls the stub; and it passes six arguments, `rom_i2c_writeReg_Mask` taking six: `e` in a7, `f` the caller's stack word read before the frame is made, scratch moved from a7 to a8/a9. `rom_call` asserts all six (`six arguments=0x00654321`).

**The emulator does not take the bring-up path** (its SYSCLK_CONF reads the PLL, so `pll80` prints `bbpll=kept`; CPU_PER_CONF and the analog master drop stores). `pll_bringup` calls the bring-up directly and asserts its timeout result and OPTIONS0's cleared bits, which cannot depend on host speed since CAL_DONE never comes there. Mutants: 12 of 12 red where the emulator can judge (P1's mask, drop, inversion and macro; P5's removal, inversion and bound; the timeout status; the stub's `e`, `f` and `f`'s slot; the key's branch). What only the part can judge: the analog words and their order, R3, P2, P3, P6, P7, the calibration and lock, the switch and the speed it gives, the IRAM placement's need, the flash at 20 MHz.

**One more change to `xtal40`:** both writing keys (in the shared `keys/clock-common/`) now take one SYSTIMER snapshot and discard it before measuring, a guess at why the part's "before" figure read 2.5% low; the board run will show whether it was the cause.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| `kill_churn.xtal40` | `sysclk=0x000a8001->0x000a8000 cpu_per=0x0000000c->0x0000000c` | 19.84 -> 40.01 | 14.165 s | 14.266 s |
| `kill_churn.pll80` | `sysclk=0x000a8001->0x000a8400 cpu_per=0x0000000c->0x0000000c options0=0x1c00a000->0x1c00a000 bbpll=0x000000c3 ana_conf0=0x2100e404` | 19.84 -> 80.01 | 7.882 s | 7.938 s |

Every image: `rst:0x1 (POWERON),boot:0x8 (SPI_FAST_FLASH_BOOT)`, `uart_clk=0x03700000 mspi_core=0x00000000 mspi_clock=0x00030103 rtc_date=0x02101271`, 200 teardown lines (201 with the last child in the two `kill_churn` images).

- **Against the predictions:** `sysclk` after is 0x000a8400, so bit 19 reads 1 on the PLL. `options0` reads 0x1c00a000 before and after: the three power-down bits were already clear, so that write left the word as it was. `bbpll` is 0xc3: OR_LOCK and OR_CAL_END both set. `ana_conf0` is 0x2100e404: bit 24 set, bit 2 set, bit 3 clear.
- **`mhz` before is still low**, 19.84 and 19.76 where 20.00 was hoped for; the earlier images read 19.46 and 19.54, so throwing the first snapshot away recovered about half of it.

## What I checked before the writes

- **The images:** sizes and SHA-256 as in your table.
- **The source:** `stub-SO-112.p2.diff` is SO-112.p2 as merged, line for line; `pll80-on-stub.diff` is SO-113.p1's lines.
- **In the emulator** (every device write traced, against a `boot` image): beyond the baseline the crystal image writes the SYSTIMER's registers and 0x600C_0060; the two PLL images add 0x600C_0010. Nothing else, and as you said the emulator takes the `bbpll=kept` path.
- **The bring-up, which the emulator does not run in those images:** I built `pll_bringup` from SO-113.p1 and traced it. It writes 0x6000_8000 (0x1c000000, after the harness set the three bits), 0x6000_E044 (0x3ff00, then 0, since the emulator reads that word back as 0), 0x600C_0010 (0x4) and 0x6000_E040 (0, 0x8, and at the end 0, 0x4). The ROM's writer then puts the seven analog words on the bus at 0x6000_E000, in IDF's order and with IDF's values: block 0x66, register 4 = 0x6B, 2 = 0x50, 3 = 0x08, 5 read and written 0 twice, 6 = 0x73, 9 read and written 3 (it also writes 0x6000_E048 and, in its masked form, 0x6000_E044). It answers the timeout, as you said it would there.
- **And read it against ESP-IDF v5.4.1**, each line in the local clone: the three bits of `clk_ll_bbpll_enable` (0x540); `regi2c_ctrl_ll_i2c_reset` and `_bbpll_enable` (ANA_CONFIG [17:8], bit 17); `SYSTEM_PLL_FREQ_SEL` bit 2; the calibration's start and stop in their order; `clk_ll_bbpll_set_config(480, 40)` word for word and in its order (0x6B; 0x50; 8; DR1 0; DR3 0; 0x73; DBIAS 3; block 0x66, host 1); the ROM's `rom_i2c_writeReg`, `_Mask` and `readReg` addresses; and the switch (`CPUPERIOD_SEL` 0, `PRE_DIV_CNT` 0, `SOC_CLK_SEL` 1).
- **One thing IDF does that the key does not:** `rtc_clk_cpu_freq_to_pll_mhz` also sets `RTC_CNTL_SLAVE_PD` when it raises the frequency. The key writes no LDO word, and `rtc_date=0x02101271` has that field 0 before and after, which is every slave on, the state the ROM leaves. I took that as the safe side and flashed; the patch's text should say it.
