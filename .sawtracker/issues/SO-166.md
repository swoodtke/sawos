**Networking K1s: the quarantine in the stats.**

The design is `SO:virtio-net` revision 4, §3.5.
- **The pool.** `Pool.Available` grows from three words to five: `pinned` and `quarantined`.
- **The process.** The stats row gains a `pinned` doubleword. A dead process's record keeps it, so a supervisor can read what a dead driver left pinned.
- **The sweep.** Every reader of the pool record and the stats row moves to the new sizes.
- **Cases.** `pool_available_pinned`, `process_stats_pinned`, and step 3 of `memory_pin_quarantine`.
