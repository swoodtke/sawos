Approved patch SO-167.p1 (revision 1)

**Approved.** The pin moves both lines together: `version = "0.24.4"` and `sha = "3c91ae48c1ad76707178c25a586fe2210c43bd3a"`, which is `v0.24.4^{commit}` in sawlang.

**The source edits** are the adaptations v0.24.4's notes call for:
- SL-1012's distinct aliases (`?? Byte(0)`, `Vector<Byte>` and the like in the image builder, the net packages and the tests);
- SL-1174's fix, which puts `///` back above private fields;
- SL-1161.p1's `blocking` for a joining `kmain`.

**The new seam:** sosrt's strong `__saw_rt_thread_yield` calls `sos_rt_yield`, a trap to the kernel's yield that answers at once where it cannot switch. It runs no program code, so it already meets v0.24.5's rule that no seam runs program code on the caller's thread.

**SL-1196's slowdown is gone with SO-169 on main.** On this tree built with v0.24.4, `arena_race` and `arena_churn` boot and finish in 0.036 s and 0.064 s under `qemu-system-riscv32` (medians of 5). That matches v0.24.3 (0.034 s and 0.073 s).

The `uart_dropped_read` failure in your first local run is SO-172's host-time dependency, seen now on two trees. It passed 10 of 10 on arm64 here, with and without SO-148.p6.
