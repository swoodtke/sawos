## Description

**Unit U3 of the GPIO/PWM drivers design (SO-77): the PWM (servo) driver.** U1 (SO-131) has merged; U3 runs beside U2. The owner accepted every recommendation of the design's §11 (Nov 16): servo pins 41 and 42, no ramp in this version.

**Build what §12's U3 lists, as U1 left the ground** (read scratchpad `SO-77/u1-as-built.md` first: where it and the design differ, it wins): `drivers/pwmproto/` (§5.4: `Set`, `Stop`, `Inspect`, `Log`), the PWM driver package and its `process.toml`, its configurations (`pwmconfig`), the smoke cases `pwm_service` (a described S3 image: the stock root with the four `[[setup]]` writes and the two parts `GPIO.out_sel[41..=42]` and `IO_MUX.pad[41..=42]`, the driver, a test client child) and `pwm_bad_config` (runner cases on the stock root with `root_plan`, `devices`, `inputs`), and the board cases P1 (nothing wired, at `rom` and at `pll80`) and P2 (one servo on GPIO 41 with its capacitor and supply). Built on `esp32s3io` (`start_pwm`, `set_pwm`, `stop_pwm`, `check_pwm_config`).

**The owner's power point (Nov 16):** the box is mains-powered and always on, and should draw little at rest. `Stop(channel)` (§5.5: `DUTY_START` clear, `IDLE_LV` 0, the line low at the frame boundary, the servo limp) is how the controller cuts the pulses once the arm is home; a later `Set` resumes. Hold both directions with a case (Stop then Set, the channel's words read back by `Inspect`), and P2 checks on the part that the servo goes limp at `Stop` and moves again at `Set`. The owner has two servos (perhaps an arm and a lid), so both channels are exercised.

**Red cases:** each of §8.4's PWM rows, as amended by `u1-as-built.md` (root's setup words are held by `stock_root` and `stock_plan.saw`, not by `pwm_service`); the patch note lists the mutants. The review standard (the owner's ruling, Nov 15) applies. The emulator does not hold GPIO OUT1/ENABLE1 writes, so a wrong `OUT1_W1TC`/`ENABLE1_W1TS` setup word shows only at P1; say so in the case's comment.

**Board:** the lead requests P1 and P2 through the reviewer under the owner's standing approval; build the images with expected text and device-write lists. P2 needs the owner's wiring: a servo on 41 (and optionally 42), a capacitor across the servo supply, a 5 V supply good for an amp, common ground, and §9's 10 kΩ pull-down from each signal to ground.

**Not in this unit:** a ramp (owner: not in this version), the box's controller and motion table (SO-78).
