## Description

**Unit U7 of the image and packaging design (SO-95): products.** After U6 an image is built from a description by `sosimage build`, but only from inside the sawos tree: the runner builds Blade and the builder, and points `.sawlang` at the toolchain. A product (the useless box is the first) lives in its own repository (the owner's ruling, Nov 13) and must build, boot and flash its images against a pinned sawos without the runner. This unit makes that work and puts a product in the gate so that a sawos change which breaks products goes red here first.

**Build what the design lists** (`SO:images-and-packaging` revision 6, approved by the owner Nov 14): rule 9.8 (a product's `tools/sawos.py` takes `SAWOS_ROOT` or fetches the pin, links `.sawos`, resolves every sawos path through that one link, writes `.sawlang`, builds Blade and `sosimage`, and runs it), rule 9.9 (`build|run|test|flash`; `flash` prints the SHA-256 through `shasum`, the board the image is for, and the `esptool` line, and runs nothing), rule 5.3 (`sawos:<path>` in an image is relative to the pinned tree), rule 9.6 (a product copied out of the tree carries no lock), §12.7 (the gate's scratch product catches a break first), and §11.8's workaround (path dependencies through `.sawos`).

**Plan first.** The design leaves these open; propose an answer for each in your plan, and return a `CLARIFICATION REQUEST:` with the list before building if any answer changes behaviour a user sees:
1. The pin's file: its name and lines (sawos has no version; `sawlang.pin`'s two-line shape is the model).
2. Where the template product lives in sawos, and what it holds (an image description, a process package with its `process.toml`, `tools/sawos.py`, the pin). The gate copies it outside the tree and builds it with `SAWOS_ROOT` naming the tree: no network in the gate.
3. What `run` and `test` do before U8: booting stays Python (§10), and judging in Saw is U8's. Say which harness code a product's `run` reaches through `.sawos`, and what `test` checks until U8.
4. Which boards the gate's scratch product builds and boots for (at least riscv32 and the S3).
5. How `tools/sawos.py` relates to `tools/toolchain.py` (a verbatim copy of sawlang's resolver, rule 9.7) without forking it.

**Cases.** The gate's scratch product built and booted with its transcript checked, on the boards you propose; each refusal of `tools/sawos.py` (no `SAWOS_ROOT` and no fetchable pin, a `.sawos` that is not a sawos tree, an unknown command) with a test; `flash`'s three lines checked for an S3 image. The review standard (the owner's ruling, Nov 15): every check, refusal and stop has a case or test that goes red when it is removed or inverted; list the mutants.

**Constraints.** Create no repository and push nothing: the box's repository is SO-78's, and the owner's to make. Nothing here fetches from the network in the gate. `tools/sawos.py` never runs `esptool` or touches a board.

**Not in this unit:** the harness in Saw (U8), the C3 smoke in Saw (U9), the box (SO-78), the drivers (SO-77).
