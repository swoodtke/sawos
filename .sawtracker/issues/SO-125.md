## Description

**Unit U2b of the image and packaging design (SO-95): the per-board values.** The machinery to raise a limit (linked slab areas, an image's quota defaults, capacities read at boot, the quota door) is built and merged; the numbers it raises are still one compiled set for every board. This unit moves the board-set numbers into each HAL and gives each board its own values, and makes the board descriptions carry the defaults.

**The design is authoritative for the numbers:** `.claude/lead/so95-design.md` (the lead's copy of `SO:images-and-packaging` revision 6) §6.4, its table and its two lists (the asserts that stay compile-time per board; the checks that become the builder's, with the kernel's stop behind them), and §6.4a. In summary, each board states in its HAL:
- `GRANT_ROW_BUDGET`: 8 on riscv32 and the C3 (PMP, hardware), 16 on arm64 and the S3 (policy); `MAX_GRANT_ROWS` derived; `MIN_GRANT_ROW_BUDGET` removed;
- `MAX_HANDLES`: 32 on every board (the C3 if its RAM allows: measure its slack and say), with the new compile-time assert `MAX_HANDLES >= 5 + MAX_MEMORIES + MAX_IOMEMORIES` and `MAX_HANDLES <= 255`;
- `PROT_DOMAIN_SLOTS`: 16 on arm64 (its tables cost 24 KiB a slot), unchanged elsewhere.
The slab floors stay as they are on every board (the suite's numbers). The per-board default slab counts and quota defaults of §6.4's table (processes, waiters, events, timers, memories, iomemories, pipes; quota mappings and handles) go into each `hal/<board>/board.toml` under `[limits.default]`, which the builder reads and turns into linked slab areas and the image's quota defaults for an image that states none of its own; the runner's cases keep building as they do today unless a case says otherwise (say how a case asks for its board's defaults, and which cases do).

**What to watch.** Raising `MAX_HANDLES` and the row budget moves numbers the suite asserts: list every case that reaches each changed number (a case that fills a handle table, a row budget, a quota row) before changing anything, and for each one say whether its expectation changes and why, from the spec. Cases that hard-code `MAX_HANDLES` as 16 because no op publishes it (`quota_set_handles`, `child-quota-launcher`) must follow; if the number should be readable by a process, say so and stop for a ruling rather than inventing an op. The spec's `(not yet enforced: limits)` markers on the per-board sentences (§2.8, §5b.1) are this unit's to make true and remove; the transition note's remaining paragraph is rewritten to what is left, if anything.

**Not in this unit:** generated link addresses (U4); the stock root (U6); lowering a limit.

**Cases**, on every gate profile and the S3 smoke: a case that reaches each board's raised numbers (a handle table filled to 32; rows to each board's budget); the per-board defaults reaching an image that states none; each compile-time assert shown to fire on a board whose numbers break it (a scratch edit, reverted); the builder's refusals of a `[limits.default]` that breaks a derivation, by name. Each new check held by a mutant.

**Lead: SO-125.p1 is up: each board's own limits, and the defaults an image gets from its board.** Rebased by the lead onto main with SO-113 (it applied with nothing to resolve) and three prose lines rewrapped. Gate on the implementer's machine (before SO-113): 1047 / 6 / 59 and 1 board-only.

**Each board's numbers, as built**

| | riscv32 / flat | arm64 | S3 | C3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `GRANT_ROW_BUDGET` | 8 | 16 | 16 | 8 |
| `MAX_GRANT_ROWS` | 8 | 16 | 16 | 8 |
| `MAX_HANDLES` | 32 | 32 | 32 | 32 |
| `PROT_DOMAIN_SLOTS` | 256 | 16 | 256 | 256 |

`MIN_GRANT_ROW_BUDGET` is removed; each HAL exports `KERNEL_RAM_BASE` and `RAM_TOP` (arm64's was private). The C3 has 45,600 B free after `.bss` with 32 handles; the S3's `root_server_boot` leaves 20,464 B, and 10,160 B with its board's defaults linked.

**One departure from the design's table, the rule kept:** the table says `MAX_GRANT_ROWS` 9 on riscv32 and the C3, but its own rule, max(budget, `MAX_ROOT_SEGMENTS` + 1), gives max(8, 7 + 1) = 8 there; 9 was today's single number, set by arm64's 8 segments. The rule is built, and a new assert holds it ("the larger of", which fires at 9). Nothing a process can observe moves: its rows are bounded by the budget, 8, on both boards. The lead accepts the rule over the table cell.

**`[limits.default]`** in each `board.toml` as §6.4's table gives it. A case asks for its board's defaults with `"limits": BOARD_LIMITS`; `sosimage defaults KERNEL BOARD` (new) plans them and prints the image's `limits`. `board_limits` (new; three gate profiles and the S3 smoke) asserts the board's slab areas, the child's quota (`handle=28`, mapping 4 or 12), the child mapping to its quota with the board's budget installed, and root's table reaching 32. The runner also plans every gate board's defaults against the shipping kernel each run (a mapping default of 5 on riscv32 failed the profile by name).

**Cases that reach a changed number**, each from the spec: `quota_set_handles` (16/17 to 32/33, §2.7), `quota_set_order` (17 to 33), `image_rows_at_capacity`/`past_capacity` (rows 41/42, Memory raise 17, so the handle table still refuses first: only the `slots=` line moves); `pipe_table_full`, `pipe_consume`, `process_records` holders grow, text unchanged; on arm64 `quota_set_rows`, `quota_set_rows_child` and `quota_default_past_rows` now link a Mapping area (root's row is clamped to the slab, §2.9), text unchanged. No op needs to publish `MAX_HANDLES`: it is 32 on every board.

**Asserts shown firing** (scratch edits, restored by hash): handles ≥ 5 + memories + iomemories (28), the ≤ 255 index bound (256), segments + stack ≤ rows, budget ≤ rows, the "larger of" (9 on riscv32, 17 on arm64), mapping default + 4 ≤ budget.

**Builder refusals with tests:** `sosimage board` now holds a kernel range's base to `KERNEL_RAM_BASE` and every range to `RAM_TOP`, from both sides and against the compiled HAL, and the two directions carried from SO-121's review (a process range starting above root's base; a kernel range ending above it). `board_defaults` refuses, naming `[limits.default]`: below a floor, records, threads or attachments below their derivation, bodies past the domain pool, a mapping quota past the free rows, a handle quota at the table or below its compiled default, a quota at its slab's capacity. `tests/board_defaults.saw` plans every board in the tree, the C3's among them.

**Mutants:** 15, each red (M2 needed the opposite-side test first): the kernel-base check, its comparison both ways, the `RAM_TOP` check and its bound, the carried conjunct and comparison, swapped fact words, `RAM_TOP` not exported, `board_defaults` planning nothing, a zero dropped from the JSON twice, and three runner mutants (defaults not written, not expanded, not fetched).

**Spec:** the "(not yet enforced: limits)" markers in §2.8 and §5b.1, §2.9's sentence, the transition note and the open item are gone; the "not held" sentences are gone from `boardcheck.saw`, `CLAUDE.md`, the runner and `main.saw`.

**Left, as the design leaves it:** the Mapping slab's 4 × processes stays a sizing rule, not a refusal. The C3 smoke does not plan its defaults against its real kernel, because the C3 section does not build the image builder on main; SO-117.p2 adds that, and the C3's defaults go into that section once both have merged.
